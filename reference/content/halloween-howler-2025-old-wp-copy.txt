WELCOME RACERS and COACHES!
CGSC and its Race Committee proudly organize over 26 regattas per year. Out of respect for our 1300 plus members, we kindly ask our guest racers, their parents and their coaches to support the OA by respecting a few guidelines:
COMPETITOR CHECK-IN UPON ARRIVAL
You must register/check-in at our dock office to be approved to store your boat on the premises in a designated space.
COACH CHECK-IN UPON ARRIVAL
You must register/check-in at our dock office to be approved to moor your vessel at CGSC. Note that only sailboats are authorized to dock. Call our launch to taxi to/from the mooring (VHF 78).
RACING DAYS RESULTS/INFO
Posted on notice board at the end of each day’s racing
REGATTA SHORESIDE RULES
No parking for non-members.
Follow the staff’s directions to unload/load.
UPON ARRIVAL
– Both competitors and coaches must register their boat with the dock office.
BOAT/DOLLIES STORAGE
– Competitors must keep both boat and dolly in their allotted space.
GEAR & EQUIPMENT
– Competitors must keep their gear & equipment in the allotted space. no bags, gear, or equipment under the clubhouse.
Central grass area must remain clear of boats, dollies, gear, and equipment. this area may be used only for rigging purposes, not storing or rinsing.
RINSING
– Hoses are conveniently placed for you on the strip and at the end of the northern and southern docks. no rinsing is permitted on the central grass area.
HELP US KEEP CGSC CLEAN
– Several trash cans have been added around the boat storage areas, please pick up trash and debris.
Only sailboats are authorized at docks.
No coach boat may be stored at the docks. only a brief “touch and go” courtesy will be allowed.
HAUNTED MOORING FIELD
Every year, on the occasion of the Halloween Howler Regatta, the CGSC members take great pride in decorating their boats and in welcoming trick-or-treaters. Our water taxis will take the trick-or-treaters from boat to boat to collect their stash of candies. To keep the fun going we even have awards for the boats participating!
• This event is open to all our registered racers, their siblings, all CGSC youth sailors and CGSC member children
• Regatta participants are pre-registered for the Haunted Mooring Field
• Racers’ siblings/kids of CGSC members must register (free) at the
• All trick-or-treaters must wear an appropriately sized life jacket to be allowed on the water taxi.
• Children under the age of 7 must be accompanied by an adult.
A suggested donation of $10 – $25 per family will help support our youth sailing program!
